A company specialises in styling and decorating venues for weddings, birthdays and anniversaries. They also provide other services like printing menu cards, guest RSVP, guest table assignments. Before an event is commissioned, the business needs information about the hosts, and event like date, venue, number of guests, time, colour scheme and type of event. Before generating an invoice for an event, props from the business inventory are added like table cloths, dance floor, backdrops, centrepieces. The company has different inventory props at different prices that can affect the cost of an event. Also selecting services can also influence the cost of the event. Develop an eloquent schema for an event venue stylist company to capture this scenario
